# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Introduction

Mukha ("the App") is a Smart Face Recognition Attendance System designed to help organizations manage employee attendance securely and efficiently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the application.

By using Mukha, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

Mukha may collect the following information:

### Administrator Information

- Full name
- Email address
- Account credentials

### Employee Information

- Full name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Attendance records

### Facial Recognition Data

Mukha collects facial images during employee registration to enable attendance verification using facial recognition technology.

Facial images are securely transmitted to a backend server where facial recognition processing is performed.

---

## Camera Permission

Mukha requires access to your device's camera to:

- Register employee facial data.
- Verify employee identity during attendance.

The camera is only activated when initiated by the user.

---

## How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used to:

- Authenticate administrators.
- Register and manage employees.
- Verify employee attendance.
- Maintain attendance history.
- Improve system security.
- Support attendance management features.

---

## Data Storage and Processing

Facial images and attendance-related information are securely transmitted to backend servers for processing.

Mukha uses reasonable security measures to protect data during transmission and storage.

---

## Data Sharing

Mukha does **not** sell personal information.

Information is shared only:

- With the organization operating Mukha.
- When required by applicable law.

---

## Data Retention

Employee information and attendance records are retained only for as long as necessary to provide attendance management services or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Organizations using Mukha may delete employee information when it is no longer required.

---

## Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to:

- Access their personal information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of personal information.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.

Requests should be directed to the organization administering Mukha.

---

## Children's Privacy

Mukha is intended for workplace attendance management and is not designed for use by children under the age of 18.
