# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to **Mukha**, a Smart Face Recognition Attendance System.

Mukha is designed to help organizations manage employee attendance securely through facial recognition technology. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using Mukha, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

Mukha may collect the following information:

### Administrator Information

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Account Credentials

### Employee Information

- Full Name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Position
- Email Address (optional)
- Phone Number (optional)

### Attendance Information

- Attendance Records
- Attendance Date and Time
- Attendance Status

### Face Embeddings

Mukha generates facial embeddings on the user's device for identity verification.

Only the generated facial embeddings are securely transmitted and stored for attendance verification purposes.

Mukha does **not intentionally store the original facial image** after the embedding has been generated.

---

## Camera Permission

Mukha requires access to the device camera for:

- Employee face enrollment
- Attendance verification

The camera is activated only when initiated by the user.

---

## How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used to:

- Authenticate administrators
- Register employees
- Generate facial embeddings
- Verify employee attendance
- Maintain attendance history
- Improve application security

---

## Data Storage

Mukha stores application data using secure backend services.

Face embeddings are securely transmitted over **HTTPS** and stored using Firebase-backed infrastructure and the application's database.

Attendance records and employee information are stored to provide attendance management services.

---

## Data Sharing

Mukha does **not sell** personal information.

Information is shared only:

- With the organization operating Mukha.
- When required by applicable law.

Mukha does not use collected information for advertising purposes.

---

## Data Security

Mukha implements reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information.

Data transmitted between the mobile application and backend services is encrypted using HTTPS.

---

## Data Retention

Employee information, attendance records, and face embeddings are retained only as long as necessary to provide attendance management services.

Organizations using Mukha may delete employee information and associated face embeddings when they are no longer required.

---

## Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to:

- Access their personal information.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of their personal information.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.

Requests should be directed to the organization administering Mukha.

## Children's Privacy

Mukha is intended for workplace attendance management and is not designed for children under the age of 18.
