# Delete Your Mukha Account

Users can permanently delete their Mukha account directly from the application.

## How to Delete Your Account

1. Open the **Mukha** application.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Go to **Settings**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Confirm the deletion.

## Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed:

- Administrator account
- Employee records
- Attendance records
- Face biometric templates used for attendance verification

## Data Retention

After account deletion, all associated personal data is permanently deleted from our systems. Data may only be retained where required by applicable laws or for security and fraud prevention purposes.
